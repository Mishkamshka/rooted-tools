Drag on empty grid to draw a tile. Drag a tile to move it, corners to resize. Shift-click to add tiles to the selection, or shift-drag on empty grid to marquee-select. Drop an image file onto a tile to fill it, or onto empty grid to place it as a new tile. Arrow keys nudge the selection, Delete removes it, Cmd/Ctrl+D duplicates it, Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes. Cut out background opens remove.bg for the selected tile \u2014 paste the result back with Cmd/Ctrl+V. Cut-out marks an image tile as black & white with no nodes. Break grid switches from whole modules to quarter steps across and half steps down.
